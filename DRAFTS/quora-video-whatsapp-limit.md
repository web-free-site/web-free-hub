**Target question (search):** https://www.quora.com/search?q=compress+video+for+WhatsApp+25MB

**Paste as answer:**

WhatsApp and email often cap attachments around 25 MB. Uploading a raw clip to a random “free compressor” sends your video to their servers.

A local-first option: [Free Video Compressor](https://free-video-compressor.web-free.site/?ref=quora) on web-free.site, which is designed to compress in the browser. Pick a preset close to your limit, export, and check the final size on your phone before sending.

If the tool struggles with a huge file, trim first or lower resolution—browser RAM is the bottleneck, not upload speed.
