About Web Free

Web Free is an independent directory of free, browser-based utilities published under the web-free.site domain. Each tool lives on its own subdomain so you can bookmark exactly what you need—PDF helpers, image editors, calculators, and the rest—without installing an app store package or creating a hub account.

We built the hub to solve a simple problem: fifty useful sites are hard to remember. The main site adds search, categories, short guides, and links to live tools with clear privacy notes. We do not claim ratings, download counts, or awards; the directory lists live tools we operate on this domain.

The hub is available in English here and in French at /fr/ for the tool listing. Legal terms and privacy practices for the whole network—including every subdomain—are on the Terms and Privacy pages linked in the footer.

All tools on web-free.site are free to use in your browser. This hub lists every live utility on the network.
