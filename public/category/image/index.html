Free image & QR tools

Photos, QR codes, memes, and logos often need quick edits before you share or print them. The Web Free image category collects browser utilities for resizing, compressing, converting formats, removing backgrounds, upscaling, and creating visual assets without desktop software.

Many of these tools keep your files on your device while work runs in the tab. That is useful on shared computers, when uploads are slow, or when you simply do not want images sent to a third-party server. Check each tool's privacy note on its detail page for specifics.

QR and barcode creation lives here too, alongside OCR that pulls text from pictures. If your task involves sound or video instead, see the audio and video categories. Calculators and document tools are linked from the hub footer when you need something else.

FAQ

Which image formats are supported?

Most image tools accept common formats such as JPG, PNG, and WebP; some add HEIC, AVIF, or SVG. Open the specific tool page for format details.

Does background removal run locally?

The background remover uses in-browser models; see its detail page for privacy notes and device requirements.
