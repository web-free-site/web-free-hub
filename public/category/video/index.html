Free video tools

Compressing a clip for messaging, recording your screen, or converting formats should not require paid software for simple jobs. Web Free video tools handle common tasks in the browser: extract audio tracks, trim and export, shrink file size, build GIFs, and convert containers to MP4.

Because video files can be large, local processing avoids upload queues and keeps footage on your machine when the tool works client-side. That reduces waiting time and limits how many copies of your content exist online.
