Steps

  1. Open Free BMI Calculator and choose adult or child mode (0–19).
  2. Enter height and weight in the units shown (metric or imperial).
  3. Read BMI and, for children, percentile context when offered.
  4. Discuss results with a clinician for health decisions—this is not medical advice.

What BMI does and does not do

BMI is a screening shortcut. It ignores muscle mass, bone density, and ethnicity-specific nuance. Use it for rough tracking, not diagnosis.

Child growth charts

Pediatric percentiles compare a child to WHO reference data. A single point matters less than the trend over time.

FAQ

Medical advice?

Athletes?
