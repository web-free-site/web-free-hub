Compress images for your website without upload

Open Image Compressor Hub details Free image & QR tools

Steps

  1. Export the largest assets from your design tool or CMS.
  2. Add files to Free Image Compressor’s batch queue.
  3. Choose WebP or AVIF when your site supports modern formats.
  4. Replace old assets on your host and re-test Lighthouse or PageSpeed.

How much to compress

Hero photos can stay higher quality; thumbnails and blog inline images tolerate stronger compression. Aim for the smallest file that still looks crisp on a phone.

Pair with resize

Downscale oversized camera photos with Free Image Resizer before compressing—pixels you never display should not ship to visitors. For how browser compression compares to desktop catalogs, read the honest comparison guide on the hub (search “image compressor vs desktop”).

FAQ

Will AVIF work everywhere?

Safari and Chrome support AVIF; provide JPG fallbacks if your stack requires them.

Can I download a ZIP?

Batch tools often export ZIP archives—check the live UI.
