How to compress a video to 25 MB for Discord

Open Video Compressor Hub details Free video tools

Steps

  1. Open the Free Video Compressor and load your MP4 or MOV file in the browser.
  2. Pick a target size preset close to 25 MB or lower if Discord still rejects the file.
  3. Start compression and keep the tab open until the progress finishes.
  4. Download the output and check the file size in your file manager before you upload to Discord.

Why Discord rejects large videos

Discord caps standard uploads on many accounts at about 25 MB (and less on mobile networks). Compressing locally avoids sending your raw clip to an unknown server and lets you retry presets without waiting on a upload queue.

Quality tips

If faces or text look soft, try a slightly higher bitrate preset and compress again. Short screen recordings often shrink dramatically with H.264 or similar browser encoders. Trim dead air first with a cutter tool if the clip is mostly static slides.

FAQ

Does compression upload my video?

The compressor is built for in-browser work; see the tool privacy note before you process private footage.

What if the file is still over 25 MB?

Lower resolution or trim length, then compress again.
