Crop, rotate, and filter a photo in the browser

Open Photo Editor Hub details Free image & QR tools

Steps

  1. Open Free Photo Editor and load your JPG or PNG.
  2. Crop to the aspect ratio you need for social or print.
  3. Rotate or straighten horizons, then apply a subtle filter if desired.
  4. Export and run Free Image Compressor before you upload to the web.

Scope

This is for quick fixes—red-eye, exposure tweaks, and crops—not layer-heavy retouching. Use dedicated software for high-end beauty work.

Keep originals

Destructive edits are hard to undo after export. Keep the camera file archived.
