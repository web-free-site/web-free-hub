Steps

  1. Open Free Online Calculator (English UI at /en/ on the live subdomain).
  2. Perform basic or scientific operations from the keypad.
  3. Bookmark the page on devices where you need quick math.
  4. Reload once while online if you want offline behavior described on the listing.

When to use this vs unit converter

This tool is for expressions and scientific keys. For miles→km or °F→°C, use Free Unit Converter instead—different job.
