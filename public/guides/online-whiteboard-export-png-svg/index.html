Online whiteboard: sketch and export PNG or SVG

Open Whiteboard Hub details Free design & drawing tools

Steps

  1. Open Free Whiteboard and pick pen colors that project well on Zoom.
  2. Sketch flowcharts, mind maps, or teaching diagrams.
  3. Use undo liberally; export before you close the tab.
  4. Download PNG for slides or SVG for further editing.

Teaching and standups

A quick whiteboard beats slide prep for architecture Q&A. Export after class to share notes.

Not collaborative

This guide covers solo drawing. Real-time multi-user boards need other products.
