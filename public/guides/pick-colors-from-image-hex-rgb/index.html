Pick colors from an image (HEX and RGB)

Open Color Picker Hub details Free image & QR tools

Steps

  1. Open Free Color Picker and upload a screenshot, logo, or photo.
  2. Click the pixel you want as your brand or UI accent.
  3. Copy HEX or RGB into your CSS, Figma, or slide deck.
  4. Save swatches in your design system doc— the tool does not host palettes.

Accessibility

Check contrast against white or black text before you ship a new button color. WCAG AA needs sufficient luminance difference.

Print vs screen

Screen HEX does not automatically match CMYK print. Convert with your print shop’s profile for packaging.
