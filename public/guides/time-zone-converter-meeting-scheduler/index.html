Steps

  1. Open Free Time Zone Converter and add your home zone.
  2. Enter the meeting time in the organizer’s zone or yours.
  3. Add participant cities (Kigali, Paris, New York, etc.).
  4. Copy the agreed UTC or local window into the calendar invite.

DST surprises

Daylight saving shifts break recurring meetings. Re-check every spring and autumn.
