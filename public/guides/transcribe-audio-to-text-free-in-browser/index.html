Transcribe audio to text free in your browser

Open Speech to Text Hub details Free developer & text utilities

Steps

  1. Open Free Speech to Text and choose microphone, file upload, or video source.
  2. Select the spoken language (English, French, or Swahili as listed).
  3. Run transcription and review the draft in the editor.
  4. Export TXT for notes or SRT for captions.

Good audio improves accuracy

Use a quiet room, place the mic closer, and avoid heavy music under speech. For interviews, separate tracks beat a single noisy MP3.

Editing the transcript

Always proofread names and numbers. Whisper handles clear speech well but can miss jargon—fix terms before you publish subtitles.
