Steps

  1. Trim the source clip to a short loop (a few seconds keeps file size sane).
  2. Load the video into Free Video to GIF.
  3. Set frame rate and width so the GIF stays under chat limits.
  4. Download and test in Slack or Discord before you post.

GIF size tradeoffs

Full HD GIFs explode in megabytes. Narrow width and lower FPS make loops shareable.

Alternatives

For product demos, consider MP4 with sound muted—many apps play video more efficiently than GIF.
