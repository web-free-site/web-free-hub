Steps

  2. Check word count against the assignment limit.
  3. Use reading time to gauge presentation length.
  4. Edit in your editor and paste again until you hit the target.

Admissions essays, scholarships, and exam sections often specify hard caps. Counting in the browser avoids sending your draft to a server.
