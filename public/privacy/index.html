Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the web-free.site network handles information when you use the hub at web-free.site and any associated tool site on a *.web-free.site subdomain (collectively, the "Site" or "network"). It applies to all tools listed in the hub directory unless a specific tool page states otherwise for a narrow feature.

What we collect

Most Web Free tools are designed to work without user accounts. We do not sell personal data. We do not offer a signup form on the hub that stores profiles in our database.

Depending on the tool and your browser, processing may happen entirely on your device (for example compressing an image or formatting JSON). When work stays in the browser, file contents often never reach our servers. Tools that need live data (such as currency rates) may fetch public information over the network without storing your inputs long term.

We may still receive limited technical information because the network is hosted online.

Advertising (Adsterra)

Some pages in the network display advertisements served through Adsterra or similar ad partners. Those companies may use cookies, device identifiers, and similar technologies to serve ads, measure performance, and limit repeat impressions. Their use of data is governed by their own policies. You can manage cookies in your browser settings and through consent tools where offered by the ad provider.

Hosting, security, and analytics

The Site is delivered through Cloudflare and related infrastructure. Cloudflare may process IP addresses, request timestamps, URLs, and security signals in server and firewall logs to provide caching, DDoS protection, and delivery. We also use Cloudflare Web Analytics (or comparable lightweight analytics) on some pages to understand aggregate traffic without relying on invasive third-party trackers for core measurements. Log data is retained for a limited period consistent with hosting practices.

We use a small reference script (ref.web-free.site) on some pages to understand when visitors arrive from the hub; this is part of operating the directory.

Children

The network is intended for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the network, contact us using the details on the Contact page and we will take reasonable steps to delete it.

Your choices

  • Use browser privacy modes and block third-party cookies if you prefer fewer ad-related identifiers.
  • Close tools after use on shared devices so local files or clipboard data are not left behind.
  • Read each tool's hub description for whether it keeps processing on your device.

Changes

We may update this policy to reflect new tools, hosting, or legal requirements. The "Last updated" date at the top will change when we do. Continued use of the network after an update means you accept the revised policy.

Contact

Questions about privacy should be directed as described on our Contact page. This policy is published on the hub so every tool subdomain can link here for a single network-wide statement.

All tools on web-free.site are free to use in your browser. This hub lists every live utility on the network.
